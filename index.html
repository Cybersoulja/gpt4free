Who we are

About Oneseco Media

Oneseco Media is an independent studio focused on AI tooling, open-source development, and digital media. We build software that removes barriers — whether that’s free access to frontier AI models, automation utilities, or content that educates and entertains.

Our work is guided by the belief that cutting-edge technology shouldn’t require a corporate budget to use.

  • 🧠
    AI & Automation Reverse-engineered APIs and automation tools that make AI accessible without API keys.
  • 💻
    Open Source Everything we build is public, forkable, and free for the community to use and improve.
  • 🌍
    Digital Media Content, podcasts, and creative projects exploring technology and culture.
